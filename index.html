About the Merit List

This portal is a student/community initiative for collecting and displaying submitted MDCAT merit information for Bajaur students. It is not an official government, KMU, or MDCAT-authority merit list — it exists to give Bajaur students a simple, shared way to check their aggregate and see where they stand.
